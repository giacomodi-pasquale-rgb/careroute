Understand the cost-access labels

Community health centers/FQHCs: Insurance is not required. Fees are adjusted using income and family size. Patients at or below 100% of the federal poverty guideline receive a full discount or may pay a nominal charge, depending on the center’s policy and service.

NJ Charity Care: This is hospital financial assistance for eligible uninsured or underinsured patients. An application and screening are required, and some separate professional, prescription, or other charges may not be covered.

Published flat fee: CareRoute shows one only when a current authoritative source confirms the exact service and price.

How CareRoute orders these options

Safety gates come first: emergency selections show only verified hospital emergency departments appropriate for the selected adult or child population. Age and published capability limits are applied next. Eligible options are then ordered by care-setting fit, access needs, and, when available, current driving duration. Unsupported wait, insurance, and cost claims are excluded.

What “verified” means

Facility identity, address, care setting, adult/child population, phone, displayed hours, listed capabilities, and access claims are tied to provider or government sources. Information can change—call before non-emergency travel.

Pilot limitations

A careful foundation, not a finished medical product.

This public pilot covers a curated set of verified adult and pediatric emergency, urgent-care, and community health-center options. It is not exhaustive, does not assess symptoms, and does not know capacity, wait time, final cost, or insurance network status. Access labels describe verified programs, not individual eligibility. Facility and government pages remain the source of truth.
