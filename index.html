How CareRoute orders these options

Safety gates come first: emergency selections show only verified hospital emergency departments appropriate for the selected adult or child population. Age and published capability limits are applied next. Eligible options are then ordered by care-setting fit and, when available, current driving duration. Wait time, insurance, cost, and composite quality scores are excluded without reliable comparable feeds.

What “verified” means

Facility identity, address, care setting, adult/child population, phone, displayed hours, and listed capabilities are tied to provider or government sources. Each card links to its sources. Information can change—call the facility and insurer before non-emergency travel.

Pilot limitations

A careful foundation, not a finished medical product.

This public pilot covers a curated set of verified adult and pediatric emergency, urgent-care, and community health-center options. It is not exhaustive, does not assess symptoms, and does not know capacity, wait time, final cost, or insurance network status. Access labels describe verified programs, not individual eligibility. Facility and government pages remain the source of truth.
