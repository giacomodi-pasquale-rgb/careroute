How CareRoute orders these options

Safety gates come first: emergency selections show only hospital emergency departments; age and published capability limits are applied next. Eligible options are then ordered by care-setting fit and, when available, current driving duration. Wait time, insurance, cost, and a composite quality score are excluded because no reliable real-time or pediatric-comparable data feed is connected.

What “verified” means

Facility identity, address, care setting, pediatric capability, phone, and displayed hours are tied to provider or government sources. Each card links to its sources. Information can change—call the facility and insurer before non-emergency travel.

Pilot limitations

A careful foundation, not a finished medical product.

This public pilot covers a curated set of nearby pediatric emergency and urgent-care options. It is not exhaustive, does not assess symptoms, and does not know capacity, wait time, cost, or insurance network status. Facility pages remain the source of truth.
