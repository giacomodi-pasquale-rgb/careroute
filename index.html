How this demo ranks options

First, it removes facilities that are closed, outside the child’s age range, lack required capabilities, or are not emergency departments when emergency care is selected. It then combines travel, appropriateness, wait, quality, and relevant capabilities into the CareRoute Pediatric Score v0.1. Scores are illustrative—not clinical advice.
